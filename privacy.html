PodCuts

Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

PodCuts (its control page is called Pith) is a personal tool run by one person on their own computers. Its only user is its operator, and it collects no information from anyone else.

No server of its own, no database of other people.

Everything the tool holds sits in files on the operator's own PC and laptop.

YouTube API Services

PodCuts uses the YouTube API Services. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. PodCuts's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

When the operator signs in with Google, PodCuts asks for three permissions on the operator's own channel:

  • YouTube Analytics (read only): views, average view duration, percentage viewed and audience retention for the channel's own videos, read about once an hour.
  • YouTube account (read only): the channel's ID and name, to check the sign-in belongs to the channel the clips are for.
  • Upload videos: to upload a rendered clip to that channel, only when the operator presses the upload button for that clip. The title, description and privacy setting are shown before it is sent.

What is stored, and where

  • Google's access and refresh tokens for the operator's channel, in a file on the operator's computers. They are sent only back to Google.
  • The channel's analytics figures listed above, stored with each of the operator's own posts and refreshed while the post is tracked.
  • Tokens for the operator's own Instagram, TikTok and Buffer accounts, used only to post to those accounts.
  • Video and audio downloaded from public sources, and the clips made from it.

Sharing

Data received from Google is used only to show the operator how their own videos perform and to upload their clips. It is not sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or combined with data about other people. PodCuts shows no ads.

Cookies

PodCuts does not place cookies on visitors' devices. This site sets no cookies and does not track visitors. The operator's own control page is behind a sign-in that uses a cookie on the operator's own browser only.

Revoking access, and deletion

Google access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page, or with Disconnect on PodCuts's Accounts page. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens straight away. The operator deletes the stored YouTube analytics figures within 7 days of disconnecting, and within 30 days of a revocation made on Google's page. Every 30 days PodCuts checks that its Google access is still allowed.

Other people's data

PodCuts does not collect, buy or process personal information about members of the public.
