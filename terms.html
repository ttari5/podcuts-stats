Last updated 23 November 2026

PodCuts (its control page is called Pith) is a personal tool, run by one person on their own computers. It cuts publicly available videos into short vertical clips and helps its operator post them to the operator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Who can use it

PodCuts is not offered to the public. There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no paid plans. Only its operator uses it, signed in to their own channels.

YouTube

PodCuts uses the YouTube API Services. By using PodCuts you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. PodCuts reads the operator's own channel's analytics and can upload a rendered clip to that same channel, only when the operator presses the upload button for that clip.

TikTok

PodCuts connects to TikTok only to upload video files to the operator's own TikTok drafts, with the operator's own authorisation. It does not read or collect other people's TikTok content and does not act for anyone else.

Content

The operator is responsible for the clips they create and post, and for holding the rights needed to post them. Clips are reviewed by hand before anything is uploaded. PodCuts does not remove watermarks or alter audio or video to avoid content matching.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind. It may stop working at any time, for example if a platform changes its API.
